The rounded-bars mark — two pill columns, short then tall: two values side by side.

```jsx
<Mark variant="tile" size={32} title="Country Comparator" />
<Mark variant="bars" size={22} color="#fff" />   {/* in the blue header */}
```

- Tile corner radius is 112/512 (21.875%). Bars are always white on the tile.
- In the header, use `bars` in white beside the wordmark; never put the tile on the blue header.
- Minimum size 16px (favicon).
